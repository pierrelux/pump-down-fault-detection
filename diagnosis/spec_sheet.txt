MANUFACTURER SPECIFICATION SHEET
=================================

Model:          ResComfort RC-36A Split System Air Conditioner
Type:           Residential split system, cooling only
Refrigerant:    R-410A
Nominal charge: 8.08 lbm (factory charge, 25 ft liquid line)

OUTDOOR UNIT (Condensing Unit)
  Compressor:     Scroll, single-speed, 3600 RPM
  Condenser:      Microchannel heat exchanger, louvered fins
  Condenser fan:  Single-speed, 900 CFM nominal
  Rated capacity: 36,000 BTU/h at 95°F outdoor

INDOOR UNIT (Evaporator / Air Handler)
  Evaporator:     Microchannel heat exchanger
  Expansion:      Thermostatic expansion valve (TXV)
  TXV superheat:  8-12°F nominal
  Blower:         Multi-speed

CONNECTING LINES
  Liquid line:    3/8" OD copper, field-installed
  Suction line:   7/8" OD copper, field-installed

NORMAL OPERATING RANGES (cooling mode, 95°F outdoor, 80°F indoor)
  Suction pressure:    120-180 psi
  Discharge pressure:  300-420 psi
  Subcooling:          8-18°F
  Superheat:           8-14°F
  Discharge temperature: 140-180°F

SAFETY CONTROLS
  Low-pressure cutout:  20 psi
  High-pressure cutout: 610 psi
